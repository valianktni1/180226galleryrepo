# Release 2.0 — Album Selection Plans

Release 2.0 adds a complete, optional album-choice workflow to each gallery.
Existing galleries remain compatible and no gallery files, shares, passwords or
existing choices are migrated or deleted during deployment.

## Admin controls

- **Off** — hides album wording and controls from the couple.
- **40 + Cover** — requires exactly 40 album photographs and one separately
  chosen cover photograph.
- **60 + Cover** — requires exactly 60 album photographs and one separately
  chosen cover photograph.
- Changing from 40 to 60 preserves all choices.
- Changing from 60 to 40 is blocked when more than 40 photographs are already
  selected; the application never silently removes a choice.
- A submitted selection can be reopened without losing it.
- The gallery records a dated history of plan changes, submission and reopening.
- Album instructions can be emailed when a couple adds an album later.

## Couple experience

- Album controls appear only on the primary full-gallery download link.
- Guest-upload and individual-folder links never show album controls.
- A live counter shows the exact number selected and the separate cover state.
- Submission is available only at exactly 40/60 photographs plus a cover.
- A final confirmation is required before submission.
- Submitted choices are locked until reopened by the administrator.

## Completed album files

Submission creates a complete revision in **Album Favourites**:

- 40 or 60 chosen album photographs; and
- one additional cover copy prefixed with `COVER -`.

The prior completed revision remains intact until the new revision has copied
successfully. Only files generated by an earlier album submission are retired;
legacy or administrator-added files are not automatically deleted.

## Verification

The release includes pure rule tests, protected-route structure checks and a
frontend release check. Deployment should build both images away from the live
containers, run the checks, and only then recreate the backend and frontend.
