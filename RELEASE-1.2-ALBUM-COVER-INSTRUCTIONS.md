# Release 1.2 - Album Cover Instructions

This small release updates customer-facing wording only. It does not alter the
working favourites, album submission, cover selection or email-notification
logic.

## Correct workflow

1. The couple hearts their favourite wedding photographs.
2. They open the favourites view and select **Submit for Album**.
3. The submitted photographs appear in **Album Favourites**.
4. They tap **Cover** on the image they want for the album front cover.
5. The application saves the selection and emails Mark automatically.

The obsolete instruction asking couples to email their cover image number has
been removed from the submission popup and gallery-ready email. The downloadable
favourites guide now includes the Cover-button step.
